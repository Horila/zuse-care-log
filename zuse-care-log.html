What you have

Type in what you have in hand — the app counts down everything logged from that day onwards, so edits, deletions and sheet pulls all correct themselves. Re-enter the amount whenever you restock. The rate is a flat divide by the chosen window, not by “days that had an entry”, so a twice-weekly tablet doesn’t read as a daily one. To use your own figure instead of the estimate, type how many he takes a day in the per-day box; clear it (on each device you use) to go back to the estimate.

Reminders

These levels go up to the Stock tab of the Google Sheet every time you sync. The Apps Script checks them each morning; once something drops under a week of supply — or, for insulin, under one bottle of 400 units — it emails you and puts an event in your Google Calendar for the day it is due to run out — so the reminder reaches your phone with the app closed. Run installStockAlerts in the script once to switch it on. Sync after restocking — the morning check reads what was last pushed, not what is on this phone.

App features & setup


  
Logging

  
Insulin, food, medication (Samylin, Prednisolone, Paracetamol, Synulox, Cerenia and more), walks, treats, bathroom and tummy-trouble notes, weight, urine tests, vet visits, low sugar, and free-text notes for anything else. The Today screen has one-tap quick-log buttons for the routine ones; Custom entry opens a full form with amount, date/time, notes and photos for everything else.


  
Your own items

  
Log tab → + New item adds anything else you give him (a new medication, say) with its own icon, unit, default amount and colour, and can track its stock. Setup → Your items edits them.


  
Safety

  
Low sugar (the 🆘 tile) opens a card with your vet's rescue steps and call buttons, and logs what you saw at one tap. Emergency vet call buttons sit at the bottom of Today; they work with no signal. After food is logged the app asks how much he ate, and the answer goes to the other phone. A dose change made on one phone is offered on the other. Sick or diarrhea twice in a day suggests a urine test, and a weight drop past your figure shows a warning. The steps, the numbers and the weight figure are yours: type them in Setup → Emergency vet. The app never suggests a dose and never stops you logging one.


  
Routines

  
Morning and night routines (Setup → Morning/Night routine) group the things due at each shot into one checklist on the Today screen, so nothing gets missed in the moment.


  
Walks & home location

  
Set your home location once (Setup → Home location) and a walk auto-ends when you're back within 2m of it, after the first 5 minutes.


  
History & Trends

  
History lists every entry, searchable and editable. Trends charts weight, vet glucose readings, urine tests, insulin, walking and tummy trouble over time, since the log started.


  
Stock

  
Track how much of an item you have in hand and since when; the app works out the daily-use rate from your own logged entries (or from the per-day figure you type in the item’s box) and predicts a run-out date. Insulin and Syringes are linked — a syringe is counted automatically for every insulin shot, never logged by hand.


  
Automatic alerts

  
These four all run from the Google Sheet script, on Google's own clock — not from the phone — because a phone stops running code the moment the screen locks. They only work once Google Sheet sync is set up below and the one-time Apps Script setup (further down) has been done.

  
1. Missed shot email — if a shot isn't logged within 30 minutes of its scheduled time, one email per missed slot, checked every 30 minutes.

  
2. Low stock email (to you) — when an item drops under 7 days of supply (Insulin instead warns at one bottle / 400 units left, since it's ordered further ahead), plus a calendar reminder on the predicted run-out day. Once per restock.

  
3. Vet reorder email — Prednisolone and Syringes additionally trigger a short email straight to the vet at 10 days of supply, asking them to reorder and to call or reply when it's ready. Once per restock; cancel it from that item's card on the Stock tab if you've already reordered elsewhere.

  
4. "Running out" warnings in the app — the home screen and Stock tab flag an item early, before either email above fires: 15 days for Prednisolone/Syringes, 7 days for everything else (or under a bottle for Insulin).

  
In the Android app, the phone notifies you itself too, no sheet needed: feeding & meds reminders, a shot not logged 30 minutes after its time, and stock about to run low. Set them in Setup → Phone notifications.


  
Data & privacy

  
Everything lives on this phone unless you turn on Google Sheet sync. Nothing is uploaded otherwise. Back up from Setup → Your data now and then.


  
One-time Apps Script setup

  
The alerts above need this done once, from the Apps Script editor (Extensions → Apps Script on your Google Sheet), after pasting the sync code in: fill in ALERT_EMAIL at the top, then run installReminders and installStockAlerts once each from the function dropdown — installStockAlerts is the one that asks for calendar permission. Any later change to the script needs a redeploy — Deploy → Manage deployments → pencil → Version: New version — or new buttons answer "unknown action".
